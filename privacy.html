What we collect

  • Purchase information: when you buy, our payment provider Stripe collects your name, email address, billing country or address and payment details. We receive your name, email, what you bought, the amount and the payment status. We never see or store your full card number.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not load third-party trackers.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To process your order, deliver the ebook and provide support or refunds: performance of a contract.
  • To keep business and tax records: legal obligation.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure: legitimate interests.

We do not send marketing emails unless you have opted in, and we do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, our payment processor. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Google, which provides our email service.
  • HMRC or other authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for UK tax purposes. We keep support emails for up to 2 years after our last contact.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
